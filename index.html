About

Attention creates visibility.

This is an instrument rather than a picture. A form is always present in the dark, but it has no colour and no surface of its own. It can only borrow light from the way you look at it.

Move slowly and the field condenses. Hold, and attention concentrates. Move quickly and it scatters. Stop, and what you saw dissolves, though the path you took remains for a while.
